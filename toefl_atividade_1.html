Grammar · Simple Present vs. Present Continuous

Rotina Diária

Choose the correct option for each sentence.

Tema: Simple Present vs. Present Continuous

1. She ___ to the gym every Monday and Thursday.

💡 Hábitos e rotinas usam o Simple Present com -s na 3ª pessoa.

2. Look! It ___ outside. Take an umbrella.

💡 Ações acontecendo agora usam o Present Continuous (is/are + -ing).

3. I ___ coffee in the morning, but today I ___ tea.

💡 Hábito geral (Simple Present) x exceção no momento (Present Continuous).

4. My brother ___ English at the university this semester.

💡 Ação temporária em curso no período atual usa Present Continuous.
