Mostrar transcrição (opcional)
Magical realism is sometimes misunderstood as simply fantasy fiction, but the two genres work quite differently. In traditional fantasy, the supernatural is clearly separate from the ordinary world, often existing in an entirely invented setting. In magical realism, however, extraordinary events are woven into a recognizably real world and treated by characters as completely normal. This technique allows authors to explore difficult political or historical themes indirectly, since the magical elements can symbolize ideas that might be harder to express through purely realistic storytelling.

Grammar · Second Conditional

Artes e Literatura

Choose the correct option for each sentence.

Tema: Second Conditional

1. If I ___ more free time, I would read more novels.

💡 Second Conditional: if + Simple Past, would + verbo base.

2. She would write a novel if she ___ the confidence to finish it.

💡 Situações hipotéticas no presente/futuro usam Past Simple na oração com "if".

3. If this story ___ true, it would completely change our understanding of the author.

💡 No Second Conditional, usa-se "were" para todas as pessoas com "be", mesmo I/he/she/it.

4. What ___ you do if you discovered an unpublished manuscript?

💡 Perguntas hipotéticas no Second Conditional usam "would".
