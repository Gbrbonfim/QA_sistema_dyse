How to Make a Sandwich

First, take two slices of bread. Put butter on one slice. Add cheese and ham on top of the butter. Don't forget the lettuce and tomato — they make it fresh! Put the other slice of bread on top. Cut the sandwich in half. Now, serve it with a glass of juice. Enjoy your lunch!
